Our Story

Beauty designed with passion since 2022

How it started

From a passion to a salon

Kele's Beauty Therapy was founded and planned by Keletso in early March 2022. It started almost like play: Keletso never went to classes and no one taught her how to do nails, plait hair, do makeup or give a massage.

It is a skill she loves and enjoys more than any other, and that passion grew into a small business serving clients in Eastbank, Johannesburg and beyond.

Today the salon offers hair washing, cutting and plaiting, makeup, nails and massage, all in one welcoming space next to Eastbank Clinic.

What drives us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower individuals by enhancing their natural beauty through personalised and innovative beauty treatments. We are committed to providing a relaxing and luxurious experience while using high-quality products and techniques to promote self-confidence and well-being.

Our Vision

To be a leading beauty salon known for exceptional service and transformative beauty experiences. We aspire to create a community where everyone feels valued and beautiful, fostering a culture of inclusivity and self-love, and to inspire our clients to embrace their unique beauty through continuous education and the latest trends.
